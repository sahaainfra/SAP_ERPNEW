## Module Summary

### HCM — Human Capital Management
**Employee Lifecycle**
- Employee groups (PERMANENT, CONTRACTUAL, RETAINER, TRAINEE, LABOUR) with payroll rules and leave entitlements driven by configuration
- Employee subgroups (MANAGEMENT, STAFF, SUPERVISOR, OPERATOR, SKILLED, SEMI_SKILLED, UNSKILLED)
- Onboarding checklist with mandatory items (safety induction, bank details, statutory enrolments)
- Competency register with enforced interlocks (operator licence, welder qualification, safety certification)
- Supervisor history with effective dating for historical reporting queries

**Geo-Attendance with Anti-Fraud**
- Mock location and developer mode detection — punch rejected with high-severity exception
- GPS accuracy threshold (50m) — flagged for verification, never accepted silently
- Device binding — unregistered device punch rejected, device change requires approval
- Duplicate punch detection within 5-minute window
- Impossible travel detection (Mumbai→Delhi in 5 minutes flagged for HR)
- Geofence matching with circle and polygon support
- Face match threshold (0.7) — below threshold routes for manual verification
- Offline punch sync without duplication, server time governs sequencing
- Missing checkout auto-flagged at end of day with notification
- Attendance feeds DPR manpower block automatically

**Labour Management**
- Labour gangs with supervisor, trade, and member tracking
- Daily labour report reconciles to attendance
- Gang productivity compared against rate-analysis norm
- Wage computation with minimum wage enforcement by state and skill category
- Payroll simulation lists changes before live run
- Retroactive salary revision computes arrears correctly
- Payroll posts with full cost-object assignment (site payroll lands on WBS, not head office)
- Wage slips deliver through communication layer as password-protected PDFs

### EAM — Plant, Machinery & Maintenance
**Daily Log with Validations**
- Hour meter monotonically increasing — lower reading refused, meter replacement with approval sets new baseline
- Total hours cannot exceed 24 in a day
- Fuel efficiency computed against norm — deviation raises exception naming machine, operator, date
- Fuel logged reconciles to stores issues, breaks reported
- Telematics divergence from manual entry flagged, not silently overwritten

**Maintenance Orders**
- Preventive maintenance auto-generated at hour/km/date threshold (whichever falls first)
- Breakdown maintenance with diagnosis, spares issue (movement 210), external service orders
- Maintenance order is a cost object — spares settle to equipment cost centre then to consuming project
- Downtime analysis with mean time between failures, mean time to repair, availability %

**Deployment & Internal Hire**
- Allocation interlocks — equipment with expired insurance cannot be allocated, expired-licence operator cannot be assigned
- Internal hire posts to project cost and equipment cost centre
- Fleet over/under recovery report reconciles monthly

---

### PRD — Production (Ready-Mix & Precast)
**Mix Design & Production**
- Mix design master with proportions per cubic metre, target slump, water-cement ratio
- Moisture correction adjusts batch water and aggregate weights
- Production flow: sales order → pour plan → batch ticket → production receipt (140) → dispatch (600) → delivery challan
- Weighbridge integration for raw material inward and dispatch
- Returned and rejected concrete tracked with reason and cost impact

**Production Reconciliation**
- Theoretical raw material from mix design proportions × volume produced (moisture-corrected)
- Actual consumption from stores issues
- Variance and variance % per material
- Cement variance beyond 5% flagged immediately
- Cost per cubic metre computes and compares against transfer/sale price with grade-wise margin

**Dispatch Control**
- Slump test required before dispatch — dispatch blocked until slump clears
- Cube casting at site with 7/28-day strength results
- Invoice for external orders, internal transfer posting for captive

### QMS — Quality Management
**Quality Planning**
- Inspection and test plan per activity and specification
- Material inspection plan linked to material Quality view
- Checklist builder configurable by QA/QC Manager, versioned like a master
- Approved makes register, material approval requests, method statements

**Inspection Lots**
- Auto-generated by event: receipt (IL-GRN), work inspection (IL-WRK), production batch (IL-PRD), source inspection (IL-SRC)
- Usage decision: accept, accept with deviation, reject, rework
- Only usage decision releases stock from quality hold — no manual override path
- Work inspection request lifecycle with consultant ageing tracking

**Testing with Interlocks**
- Cube tests with automatic 7/14/28-day due dates
- Calibration expiry blocks test entry
- Welder qualification interlock for NDT-critical joints
- Failed 28-day cube automatically raises non-conformance linked to pour, batch, challan, measurement
- Measurement for quantity with failed test blocked from billing

**Non-Conformance**
- Workflow: RAISED → ASSIGNED → ROOT_CAUSE → CORRECTIVE → PREVENTIVE → VERIFICATION → CLOSED
- Severity classification (MINOR, MAJOR, CRITICAL)
- Cost of rectification captured
- Ageing and escalation on SLA breach
- Repeat-cause analysis
- Linkage to vendor rating and subcontractor performance

**Quality Analytics**
- First-pass yield
- Rejection % by vendor and material
- Cube pass rate by grade, plant, month
- Non-conformance ageing and recurrence
- Cost of poor quality by project
- Inspection request turnaround with consultant

### EHS — Environment, Health & Safety
**Safety Operations**
- Induction register — no site access without induction (enforced at attendance layer)
- PPE issue register linked to returnable stores issue
- Toolbox talks with attendance capture
- Training with validity

**Permit to Work**
- Types: HEIGHT, CONFINED_SPACE, HOT_WORK, EXCAVATION, ELECTRICAL, LIFTING, ROAD_CLOSURE, NIGHT_WORK
- Issuer, receiver, validity window, precondition checklist
- Gas testing for confined space (required and must be safe)
- Risk assessment
- Interlock: permit cannot be issued to person with expired safety certification
- Closure with verification

**Incident Reporting**
- Severity: NEAR_MISS, FIRST_AID, MEDICAL_TREATMENT, LOST_TIME, FATATLITY
- Investigation with root cause (5-why or fishbone)
- Corrective action, verification, closure
- Severity-based escalation — fatality escalates to management immediately
- Days lost tracking

**Safety Statistics (Computed, Never Typed)**
- Man-hours worked from attendance
- Lost time injury frequency = LTI × 1,000,000 ÷ man-hours
- Severity rate = days lost × 1,000,000 ÷ man-hours
- Safe man-hours since last LTI
- Near-miss reporting rate (LOW rate is a warning, not a success)
- Toolbox talk coverage %, PPE compliance %, permit closure %

**Environment**
- Waste register with disposal manifests
- Water consumption and recycling (especially batching plants)
- Fuel and electricity consumption feeding emissions estimate
- Noise and dust monitoring against consent conditions
- Tree cutting and compensatory plantation records

## Key Business Rules Enforced

1. **Competency interlocks** — expired operator licence blocks equipment assignment, expired welder qualification blocks critical-joint assignment, expired safety certification blocks permit issue
2. **Anti-fraud attendance** — mock location, developer mode, GPS accuracy, device binding, duplicate punch, impossible travel all detected and acted upon
3. **Append-only attendance** — correction never overwrites original punch, both remain visible with reason and approvals
4. **Minimum wage enforcement** — wage below notified state minimum for skill category cannot be saved
5. **Cost-object assignment** — site payroll lands on correct WBS, not head office
6. **Monotonically increasing hour meter** — lower reading refused, meter replacement with approval sets new baseline
7. **24-hour limit** — total hours (work + idle + breakdown + standby) cannot exceed 24 in a day
8. **Fuel efficiency exception** — deviation beyond norm raises exception naming machine, operator, date
9. **Preventive maintenance auto-generation** — at hour/km/date threshold, whichever falls first
10. **Allocation interlocks** — equipment with expired documents cannot be allocated, expired-licence operator cannot be assigned
11. **Moisture correction** — adjusts batch water and aggregate weights, flows into reconciliation
12. **Cement variance flagging** — beyond 5% flagged immediately in production reconciliation
13. **Slump test before dispatch** — dispatch blocked until slump clears
14. **Usage decision only route** — only usage decision releases stock from quality hold, no manual override
15. **Calibration interlock** — expired calibration blocks test entry
16. **Failed cube auto-NCR** — 28-day cube failure automatically raises non-conformance linked to pour, batch, challan, measurement
17. **Permit certification interlock** — permit cannot be issued to person with expired safety certification
18. **Fatality escalation** — fatality escalates to management immediately
19. **Safety statistics computed** — from attendance man-hours, never typed
20. **Near-miss reporting** — LOW rate is a warning, not a success
